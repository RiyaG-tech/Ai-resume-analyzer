Job Title: Senior Data Scientist / ML Engineer
Company: TechNova Innovations
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote

About the Role:
We are looking for an experienced Senior Data Scientist to join our Core AI team. You will lead the development of state-of-the-art machine learning models, predictive algorithms, and NLP systems that power our customer intelligence platform.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models for classification, regression, and ranking.
- Develop and optimize scalable NLP pipelines using Transformers, PyTorch, and Hugging Face.
- Work closely with data engineers to build distributed data pipelines using Python, SQL, and Apache Spark.
- Deploy, monitor, and maintain production ML microservices using Docker, FastAPI, and AWS.
- Collaborate with product management and business stakeholders to translate strategic business objectives into quantitative ML metrics.

Required Qualifications:
- Master's or Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or related technical field.
- 3+ years of professional experience in applied Machine Learning and Data Science.
- Expert proficiency in Python and standard ML libraries (Scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, SciPy).
- Strong experience with Deep Learning frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Hands-on experience with NLP, LLMs, and RAG architectures.
- Solid understanding of SQL and relational / NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB).
- Familiarity with containerization (Docker), version control (Git), and Cloud platforms (AWS or Azure).
- Excellent communication and analytical problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with Kubernetes and MLOps tools (MLflow, Kubeflow).
- Experience with PySpark or BigQuery for large-scale data processing.
